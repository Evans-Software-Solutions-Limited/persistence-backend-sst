Relay's strongest relevant lesson is reducing migration and authoring effort. Persistence can apply that to both coaches and self-managed Premium Plus athletes, then close the loop with the same understandable progress evidence in coach and athlete views. This is a product recommendation, not a claim of uniqueness or superiority.

## Three different jobs, one connected experience

1. **Bring my existing plan:** photo, PDF, URL, pasted text or dictated instructions → source-backed draft → resolve uncertainties → save a workout/programme. Preserve what was prescribed.
2. **Help me design a plan:** explicit generation mode → goals, schedule, equipment and coach rules → proposed draft with assumptions → review. Never infer this permission from an unreadable scan.
3. **Show how it is going:** scheduled versus completed work, progress and a grounded summary → athlete and coach see the same shared evidence → comments/actions. AI does not assign an opaque adherence score.

Example: “Four weeks, Monday squat three sets of five at 80 kilos; add 2.5 kilos weekly. Friday deadlift, three sets of five at 100. No change week four.” The draft should expose whether the final sentence applies to one lift or both. It must not invent a deload, repeat week-one loads for four weeks, or silently substitute a similarly named exercise. A rough handwritten photo follows the same review rules.

## Material architecture findings

- **Scheduling is the critical prerequisite.** Current programmes repeat an ordered workout cycle and spread sessions mathematically. They do not faithfully represent arbitrary source weekdays, variable weekly sessions, rest days and week-specific prescriptions. Versioned explicit schedule entries are required for full-fidelity imports; preserve existing repeating-cycle behaviour.
- **Self-managed authoring is a permission change.** Current programme create/assign routes are trainer-oriented. Premium Plus needs owner/self endpoints and capability gates, not a trainer role, client roster or fake relationship to itself.
- **Adherence needs one definition.** Existing coach adherence and athlete gym-target progress use different denominators. A new shared service must distinguish plan adherence, gym habit attainment and data completeness.
- **Coach summaries are not automatically safe to share.** Existing client responses include coach notes and AI summaries are coach-facing. Construct an explicit shared input/output projection and keep private notes out of prompts as well as output.
- **The old import draft is stale.** Current repositories use transactions; the earlier “pooler means no transactions” rule must not guide implementation. Do not silently clamp extracted numbers, share private source caches, or grant import through a missing entitlement switch case.

## Build implications

The responsive web workspace, browser file uploads and browser microphone capture can ship independently of native binaries. Mobile image capture can reuse the installed image picker/camera after permission-copy review. The current app has neither a document-picker nor audio-recording dependency: integrated PDF file selection and in-app microphone capture will likely add native modules/configuration and require Brad's new iOS/Android build. System keyboard dictation into a text box is a useful early input, but is not the complete managed voice workflow. No build is started by this scope.

A transcription service converts audio to text; structured extraction, exercise resolution and user review are separate stages. [Amazon Transcribe](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/transcribe/latest/dg/what-is.html) supports batch and streaming, including London/Ireland. Evaluate it as a candidate alongside the existing regional AI architecture; no provider, prices or performance claims are selected without measurement. [Expo SDK 55 audio](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v55.0.0/sdk/audio/) documents recording and native permission configuration.

## Recommended order and success tests

1. Agree the capability/shared-view matrix; label representative source plans and exercise exports; prove scheduling round trips.
2. Deliver shared draft/confirm and self-authoring foundations; build web workspace and athlete review against those contracts.
3. Enable each input after its accuracy/cost gate passes, including voice. Integrate bulk exercise migration and shared progress summaries.
4. Pilot with five coaches and five self-managed athletes (proposed, not recruited): measure end-to-end time including corrections, critical errors, saves and subsequent logged workouts. Compare to each participant's manual baseline. Aim for at least 50% lower median authoring time without critical silent changes; report small-sample uncertainty and individual failures.

The hard work is prescription/schedule fidelity and safe shared access, not rendering an upload button. Image/PDF/URL/voice are medium-to-high complexity on a shared foundation; faithful multiweek scheduling and a robust desktop editor are high-complexity changes. A calendar estimate should follow the evaluation and model spike, not pretend those unknowns are resolved.

## Open product choices

Working default: all coached clients can see shared coach feedback/progress regardless of their personal tier; Premium Plus unlocks independent planning/import and self-generated summaries. A clarification was requested; this remains a recommendation until answered. All paid coach tiers are included in the requested import scope, explicitly including entry-level coach subscriptions; this differs from current Loadout eligibility and does not change Loadout automatically.

Coach-private notes stay private; shared facts and athlete-safe summaries remain visible without a separate “publish” action. Existing private summaries are not retroactively exposed. New external AI connectors, autonomous assignment, paid marketplace, wearable integrations and branded white-label apps remain future options.
