# Meta AI and creator tools — opportunities for Persistence

Research updated 15 September 2026. Based on Brad's Meta Festival transcript and official Meta sources. This is the tools/opportunity guide; the separate [conversion review](./CONVERSION-REVIEW-2026-09-15.md) covers the current funnel and Shipaton experiment. No account changes, outreach, paid campaigns or creative publication have been performed.

## The opportunity

There are useful tools here for making more effective creative, finding relevant people to demonstrate Persistence, and turning those demonstrations into measurable campaigns. We can prepare creative and investigate tools alongside fixing measurement. Tracking is a requirement for judging a paid test, not a reason to postpone all creative work.

Recommended first combination: **real app demonstration + a small set of AI-assisted creative variants + a clearly matched destination**. Then test a relevant coach/creator version of the same product promise. These are recommendations, not proven outcomes for Persistence.

## 2. Additional AI features found in current official material

These are additional desk research, not a claim that every feature was explained in Brad's webinar. Availability in Persistence's account has not been inspected.

### Creative production

Meta's October 2025 announcement covers persona-based image variants, generated music, video dubbing and AI-enhanced CTA stickers, with some capabilities still described as tests. [Official creative-tools announcement](https://about.fb.com/ltam/news/2025/10/performance-basado-en-ia-para-hoy-y-el-futuro/).

**Our application:** explore distinct customer situations—planning a session in an unfamiliar gym, recording progress, and following a coach's programme—rather than several cosmetic versions of the same ad. Pick one situation for each controlled test. Dubbing is lower priority for an initially UK-focused campaign. Music and visual enhancements should support, rather than obscure, the product demonstration. Preview all placements before use.

### Brand-aware creative and creator workflow

Meta's June 2026 announcement describes a creative workspace under testing, Brand Memory, expanded text generation including text on images, more translations and an approval-flow test. It also announces a unified Meta Creator Marketing Hub for later in 2026, Facebook creator discovery, creator-content recommendations and paid-performance insights. These are announced capabilities, not confirmed account access. [Official Cannes 2026 announcement](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2026/06/cannes-lions-2026-novas-ferramentas-criativas-e-de-criadores-para-todos-os-profissionais-de-marketing-cruzarem-a-fronteira-da-ia/).

**Our application:** prepare a reusable source pack now: approved logo/colors, app recordings, three concrete product benefits, exact current offer terms and prohibited claims. If Brand Memory/workspace access is present, that pack provides a consistent starting point. Keep generated app UI, fabricated reviews and unsupported fitness outcomes out of the assets. Creator-performance comparison is potentially valuable once there are real comparable results.

### Business messaging

The June announcement also discusses Meta Business Agent for customer conversations. **Our application:** first collect the actual questions preventing purchase—what the app does, which tier is needed, how access activates—then decide whether messaging assistance is warranted. A small approved FAQ may solve the immediate problem without an agent integration. This tool is separate from ad creative automation; it should not receive private workout or health data. [Meta's announcement](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2026/06/cannes-lions-2026-novas-ferramentas-criativas-e-de-criadores-para-todos-os-profissionais-de-marketing-cruzarem-a-fronteira-da-ia/).

## 3. A useful first evaluation

1. **Inspect access:** record the exact creative options and creator tools visible in this ad account, their eligibility, and any test/rollout labels. A newsroom announcement does not establish availability.
2. **Choose the job:** one released feature, one intended customer, one desired action. Keep founding web purchases separate from app installs.
3. **Prepare assets:** use Brad's real recording and approved source pack. Draft two or three variants for review; no auto-publication. New Train Together functionality is not advertised as available before release.
4. **Choose one test:** compare the original with one AI-assisted variation while holding the destination, offer and other important settings constant. If several enhancements change together, label it a package comparison.
5. **Read the result:** use destination/checkout outcomes or verified install/activation measures, as appropriate. Compare production effort too: creative time saved is useful even before a performance lift is demonstrated.
6. **Test creator fit next:** seek a permissioned demonstration from a suitable coach or training pair after agreeing the brief, rights, period and fee. That is proposed work; no creator has been contacted.

Possible questions: Does a visible product demonstration generate more qualified visits than a founder-led offer? Does a relevant creator improve activation compared with the same promise from Brad? Do AI variants save production time while maintaining truthful, legible creative? Do not try to answer all three with one tiny campaign.
