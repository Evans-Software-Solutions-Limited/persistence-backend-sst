## Deliverables

1. A source-linked feature matrix covering Advantage+ creative/automation, opportunity score, creator marketplace/partnership ads, conversion/brand lift, brand memory and creative workspaces, image/text/video variations, dubbing/music and any webinar-only tools with weak verification. Separate webinar claims, official announcement date, account availability and recommended use. Verify current claims against official Meta sources. If authorized account access exists, inspect read-only; otherwise mark account status unverified.
2. A brand asset checklist and draft creative pack using existing approved Persistence assets: real app demonstration, training-partner problem/benefit concept and a coach-discovery concept explicitly labeled upcoming while unshipped. Never present future features as downloadable now. Include hook, spoken copy, on-screen text, CTA, destination and factual claim evidence for each. Do not fabricate testimonials or publish generated product screens as real UI.
3. An offer reconciliation sheet: current live checkout/store terms versus draft claims, distinguishing fixed-term founding purchase from recurring app subscriptions. Report discrepancies; do not change live pricing or reuse old renewal wording without verification.
4. One bounded experiment proposal: hypothesis, audience, control/variant, single main variable, primary outcome, diagnostic measures, attribution limits, proposed duration/sample stopping rule and a proposed spend ceiling awaiting explicit approval. Choose install/use or founding purchase as the primary journey. Avoid judging success by video views alone; if downstream measurement is missing, make instrumentation the prerequisite.
5. Optional creator shortlist with public source links, fit rationale, indicative questions and draft outreach copy. Do not contact anyone, buy rights or imply consent. Brand assets, creator permissions and account eligibility must be checked before activation.
