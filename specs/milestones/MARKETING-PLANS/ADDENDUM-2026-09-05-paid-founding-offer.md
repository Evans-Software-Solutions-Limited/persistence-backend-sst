## Amendment text for `specs/milestones/FOUNDING-OFFER/BRIEF.md § 2` (append under the 2026-09-04 amendment)

> ### 2026-09-05 amendment — paid web founding offer (supersedes the 2026-09-04 wording on prices and purchase flow)
>
> The founding offer is sold on the website for a fixed period ending 30 September 2026: Premium £30 / 6 months or £60 / 12 months; Premium+ £50 / 6 months or £100 / 12 months. Payment is taken by Stripe Checkout in one-off payment mode; fulfilment is a `founding` grant created by the `checkout.session.completed` webhook through `FoundingGrantService.grant`, so redemption, email confirmation, seat caps, the live-store-subscription refusal and revocation behave exactly as reviewed on 2026-09-04. The public page therefore **does** present fixed prices and a purchase flow. Administrative grants (`founding` for cash taken in person, `complimentary` for discretionary access) continue unchanged as the second route. Contributions recorded on web-checkout grants are the Stripe amount and payment reference. Referral codes remain attribution only. In-app introductory offers at the same prices are configured in App Store Connect and Google Play as a separate path; nothing inside the mobile app references the website offer.

## Work packages (the full specifications were delivered to session A as the 5 Sep course correction; summarised here)

**WP10 — founding web checkout.** `founding_checkout_sessions` (holds a seat for 30 min while open); `POST /founding/checkout` (Turnstile, honeypot, rate limit, pool check under the pool advisory lock, Stripe Checkout Session in payment mode with `customer_email` fixed, metadata, 30-min expiry, terms acceptance, custom text, no adaptive pricing); webhook handlers `checkout.session.completed` (grant + invite, idempotent, `needs_review` path when a live `rc_*` row blocks the grant) and `checkout.session.expired` (release hold); `charge.refunded` extended to revoke the linked grant; `GET /founding/checkout/:sessionId/status` (masked email); `'stripe_checkout'` added to payment methods; `analytics_events` `checkout_started` → `InitiateCheckout` and `purchase` → `Purchase`; `/founding/thanks` route; Home banner; closed state; admin visibility of `needs_review`.

**WP11 — entitlement-aware onboarding (mobile, OTA).** Resolve `useMySubscription()` before the journey renders (that read applies pending grants); non-free tier → `account_only` mode: profile pages then a confirmation instead of the subscription step; re-check on the recommendation page; track `onboarding_mode`.

**WP12 — paywall renders pay-up-front introductory prices.** `IOSPurchaseFlowPresenter/Container` currently handle trials (`introTrialDays`); add the pay-up-front intro case (price, period, renews at standard) and the Play equivalent.

**WP9 — sales page.** Implement `LANDING_PAGE.md` v2 verbatim: hero, what you get, why now, price cards (2 × 2), inline pre-checkout step, how it works, FAQ, closing CTA, closed state, thanks page, banner; Privacy and Terms web-copy updates (Terms text from the solicitor).
