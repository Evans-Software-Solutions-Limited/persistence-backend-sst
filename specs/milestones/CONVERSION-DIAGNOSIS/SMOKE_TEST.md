# Conversion diagnosis — release evidence

Run against an authorized test environment. Record commit, environment, interval, time zone and source receipt time. Do not include personal identifiers in the report.

- Admin can view an existing plan; unauthenticated/non-admin callers cannot; foreign or absent plan/channel is rejected or marked unavailable according to the contract, never silently counted.
- Both journeys show every defined milestone. Missing source is unknown; a verified zero is zero. One failed source leaves other results visible.
- Selecting TikTok does not turn all-source registrations into TikTok conversions. Manual figures from another interval stay contextual.
- Repeat events count according to the source inventory; a zero denominator has no rate. UTC boundary membership is stable when display time zone changes.
- Late checkout confirmation and offline workout sync are reflected according to their documented cohort/cutoff rules. Purchase before workout does not imply data corruption.
- Synthetic fixture screenshots are labeled and separated from real results. No account or health data appears in aggregate response/log output.
- With Brad-authorized test access, follow the actual campaign destination to its intended outcome; compare source receipt and authoritative confirmation. If access, dates or destination are missing, record the gap and leave operational verification pending.
- Reconcile creative with actual offer duration, price, renewal and destination before proposing the next experiment. Record what is observable, what remains unknown and one measurable next step. No spend/publication is part of this smoke test.

Pass requires backend contract tests, frontend tests and rendered review. Operational measurement additionally requires real source receipt evidence; implementation passing alone does not prove attribution or conversion uplift.
