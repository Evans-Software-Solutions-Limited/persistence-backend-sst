- Building/brainstorming/posting **before** Aug 1 is explicitly allowed. TestFlight/dev is not a "release."
- **Updates to already-released apps do NOT qualify.**

- **Time the public launch to Aug 1** so the entire trainer-recruitment + content push (see `marketing/LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK.md`) happens *inside* the judging window. Launch traction = competition traction.

- Use **RevenueCat dashboards** as the evidence base (installs, trial→paid, MRR by tier) — exactly the metrics the Grand Prize and HAMM judges want.
